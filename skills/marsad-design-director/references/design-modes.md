# Requirements by design task

## Logo or product mark

Learn the exact name, capitalization, function, parent-brand relationship, likely placements, and supplied type/color rules. Form distinct directions from the name and product behavior rather than a generic industry symbol. When several names are explored together, compare the concepts before rendering: a different word in the same line-and-dot icon is not a different direction. Test silhouette, monochrome, reduced size, icon-only crop, and wordmark spacing. A parent brand may supply colors/type without forcing identical symbols. Check whether the mark remains recognizable without a descriptive subtitle. Supply the actual requested variants and verify each export opens.

## Social post, cover, and ad

Lead with the requested message and the surface where it appears. Choose among imagery, type, proof, or diagram according to what helps the viewer understand it. Use brand rules for continuity but vary composition when the message changes. For a campaign series, identify which traits should remain consistent and which should respond to each post; do not duplicate the same photo-left/text-right, card, cutout, or badge by default. Check mobile crop and readable copy; verify current platform requirements if they affect production. Do not fabricate urgency, claims or social proof.

## Explainer diagram or infographic

First map the real entities, relationships and labels. Lay out connectors so the relationship is clear and endpoints are unambiguous. Keep labels entirely inside their container or deliberately outside with clearance. Ensure connector lines, borders, circles, captions and arrows do not cross or collide with text. A schematic can be centered, asymmetric, linear or layered depending on the actual information. Use exact vector/text construction for technical content.

## Web visual or interface

Use the appropriate site or product implementation skill. Respect the existing design system and UI states. Distinguish decorative image from interactive control, and verify responsive behavior, real copy, accessible contrast, keyboard order and functional links. Do not turn every concept into the same hero plus feature-card grid.

## Presentation, print or multi-format campaign

Use the relevant document/presentation/print workflow for export. Decide what remains constant across formats: logo, type roles, palette, imagery treatment, headline voice. Recompose for each aspect ratio and viewing distance rather than scaling one layout. Check bleed and print settings when applicable.
