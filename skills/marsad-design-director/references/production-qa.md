# Rendered-output quality gate

Inspect the actual exported or rendered artifact. A clean prompt, SVG source, or HTML tree is not proof that the design looks correct. Review at full resolution and its intended size; for social, include a phone-sized or thumbnail view.

## Geometry and alignment

- Define intended alignment relationships: centered on canvas, aligned to text baseline, equal outer margins, or deliberately offset. Do not assume mathematical centering equals optical balance.
- Check each visible object's bounds against the canvas and its group. No headline, caption, icon, connector, circle, card or border may collide accidentally. A caption below a circular mark needs measurable clearance from the mark and connector.
- Check padding on all sides; inspect the actual longest line and bilingual text, not placeholder copy. Verify RTL order, Arabic shaping, punctuation and mixed numerals.
- For a logo lockup, inspect symbol-to-wordmark gap, cap height, baseline, visual center and weight. Inspect both horizontal and square variants independently.
- For diagrams, confirm connector endpoints and label placement are visually unambiguous. For UI exports, test responsive breakpoints and clipping.

## Accuracy and files

- Compare required colors, fonts, logo, copy, product details, prices, dates and links with the brief. An unapproved accent is a brand error even if attractive.
- Open every advertised PNG/SVG/PDF or editable file. Check that links target real files, transparency is correct, SVG viewBox contains the artwork, and raster resolution matches the promised use. Do not leave broken-image icons in a delivered preview.
- Test monochrome and small size for logos. Test social artwork in realistic crop and at feed size. Avoid a final result whose essential text is too small to read.
- If generated imagery is used, inspect artifacts and factual implications. Replace or repair visible mistakes.

## Decision

Treat wrong facts, brand deviations, broken exports, accidental overlaps, cropped essentials, and unreadable text as blocking. Fix them, rerender, and inspect once more. Treat subtler optical imbalance or decorative excess as revisions to make when they affect the result. If a tool prevents an exact fix, describe the limitation clearly and deliver the best honest artifact.

When comparing a set, keep actual brand constants while checking diversity of idea, silhouette and composition. Consistency should be traceable to confirmed rules, not to the skill's favorite arrangement.
